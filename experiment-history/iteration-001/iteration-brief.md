This iteration targets two Priority 1 (Document Structure) gaps and one Priority 2 (Text Content Fidelity) gap observed across the corpus. The sparc_overview paper is missing its Section 4 heading and all three italic-formatted subsections (4.1–4.3) because the postprocessor's plain-header regex requires a trailing blank line (which this heading lacks) and has no pattern for italic numbered headers. These are fixable with targeted regex additions. Separately, 8 broken-ligature words across helios_design and hawker_2020 (e.g., "feld" for "field") represent a text fidelity regression that a dictionary-based repair can address. Finally, per the progressive challenge rules, FILE_1798.pdf (241-page CERN Energy Amplifier report) is added to the corpus. Success is measured by: sparc_overview heading count rising from 6 to ≥10, broken-ligature count dropping to 0, the new paper extracting successfully, and all existing corpus tests continuing to pass.
