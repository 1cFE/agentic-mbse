# Iteration 1 Brief

This iteration focuses on establishing a clean measurement foundation and fixing the most impactful heading detection gap. The comparison report reveals that the delene_2001 baseline is contaminated with 25+ Claude Layer 3 hallucination artifacts (AI conversation text injected during failed equation repair), creating a misleading -6.2% character "regression" that is actually an improvement — the current pipeline extracts the actual table data correctly. Cleaning the baseline eliminates this phantom regression and gives us trustworthy metrics going forward. The heading detection gap — bold all-caps section headings like `**ABSTRACT**`, `**CONTENTS**` falling through both the allcaps promoter (requires no bold markers) and the unnumbered bold promoter (requires 14+ chars) — is a concrete, fixable issue affecting delene_2001 and potentially other papers with similar formatting. Finally, per the progressive challenge rules, the SPARC tokamak paper (25 pages, has tables + math) is added from the pool to expand corpus coverage from 5 to 6 papers. Success is measured by: delene heading count rising from 23 to 25+, no regressions on other papers, and all 6 corpus papers extracting successfully.
