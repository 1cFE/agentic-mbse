Second, 14 subsection headings in helios_design (3.1–3.7, 4.1–4.7) that use italic-wrapped numbering (`_3.1. Title_`) will be promoted to proper markdown headings via a new regex pattern, giving the paper a proper two-level hierarchy instead of its current flat H2-only structure. The PDF pool is exhausted (both pool files already in corpus), so no new paper is added this iteration. Success is measured by: energy_amplifier heading count dropping from 106 to ≤45, helios_design heading count rising from 7 to ≥20 with H3 entries, and zero regressions across all other papers.
