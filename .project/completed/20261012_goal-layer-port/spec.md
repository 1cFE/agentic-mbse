Wording is design's, and design works from MR-7's text, not from this summary; SC6's sentence-level crosswalk is the completeness check.
  - Keep physical relationships and performance limits separate from policies that choose a design. A calculation or binding never silently removes a design choice by setting installed equipment, inventory or capacity from required performance. Replacing one hidden fixed choice with another does not fix it.
  - Each analysis states which quantities it specifies and which it solves for, with their authority. An existing calculation direction, reference design or quality target is not that authority.
  - Evaluate the supplied design and report insufficiency instead of enlarging it. An authorized sizing or search policy stays separately identifiable, and its selected design is evaluable without rerunning it.
  - Document how a routine's calculation direction relates to the supported analysis. The rule does not require an acausal solver, or that every related quantity be specifiable at once. Physical balances, geometry identities and operating-point calculations are not automatically violations; they state their chosen quantities.
  - Report empirical validity separately from physical adequacy. An unsupported evaluation is never reported as a pass.
  - A supplied equipment choice stays the basis of its inventory and cost. A demand-scaled cost proxy says what it assumes.
  - Record affected quantities, units, roles, binding locations and authority, in proportion to the change. Call out a parameter that leaves the public interface or becomes a calculated binding. Defaults, mode switches and multipliers must not hide automatic adequacy.
  - Design review inspects the actual equations, bindings and downstream consumers.
  - Acceptance exercises an insufficient and a sufficient supplied design, or states why those tests do not apply. Where the interface lets demand vary with hardware held fixed, that is tested separately. Compliance is recorded as compliant, violated or unverified, and unknown compliance does not count as completed remediation.
