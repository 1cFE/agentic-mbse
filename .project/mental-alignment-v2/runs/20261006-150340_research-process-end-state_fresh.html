End-state research process

Summary

  • The model: research is two stages with a wall between them; acquisition proves a source is in the repo, and reading reasons only from sources already proven in.
  • What ships: one skill (research-acquire, a prompt file) plus four CLI subcommands (agentic-mbse research open | log | close | register), installed by agentic-mbse init like everything else. Reading (/research, pm approve-research) ships today and does not change.
  • The LLM does exactly three things: picks the queries, calls each hit keeper / rejected / failure, and writes three sentences per keeper. Scripts do the rest, and the result class is computed from files the scripts wrote.
  • Two decisions open: the fate of /manage-sources, and whether a goal may delegate insight approval.

The call stack

Three LLM processes: the goal agent, the researcher subagent, the /research subagent. Every write to knowledge/ is a script call from inside the researcher subagent.

The four outcomes

close asks four questions in order; the first yes wins. The right column is the goal round's next step (this model's proposal).

Text equivalent: any registered receipt, or a duplicate on a keeper: REGISTERED, goal round marks COMPLETE and schedules reading. Else any refused receipt or a failure logged as queued: OPERATOR_QUEUE, OWNER_GATE. Else a fault with no receipt: BLOCKER, PREREQUISITE. Otherwise BOUNDED_NEGATIVE, writing a negative; the round records BOUNDED_NEGATIVE. Always writes return.json.

Decisions needed

  1. Fate of /manage-sources. Keep it for sources that need no capture (a codebase, a person); verify flags an index block with no manifest row. Lean: keep.
  2. Can a goal delegate insight approval, with pm approve-research recording the approver? Open. Yes: unattended goals, with agent-approved insights. No: the owner is the only approver, and unattended goals stall at each research task.

Rules the scripts enforce
  1. Nothing is read that is not registered, and nothing is registered except by agentic-mbse research register; a fetched page proves reachability, never a fact.
  2. The class comes from receipts, never from the agent's report. A registered receipt makes the class REGISTERED whatever the agent says, and a claim with no receipt counts for nothing.
  3. A refused registration is a receipt too. So a hold-out hit, a failed download or missing metadata queues the candidate for the owner instead of vanishing.
  4. A source's identity is the SHA-256 of its raw bytes: the same paper at a second URL is a duplicate, which still answers the request but costs no capture.
  5. The registry writes four files or nothing: source directory, manifest row, index block, receipt, under one lock.
  6. A search that finds nothing within its limits is an answer. A negative is recorded under the request key, and the question will not reopen without a stated reason.
  7. Only the approval gate mints an insight; a goal that wants a DI must pass a document through pm approve-research.
  8. The goal layer maps the class to a task outcome and never defines classes; a round that finds itself repairing the seam returns PREREQUISITE instead.
The register refusal ladder

register runs its checks top to bottom, and every exit, refusal or success, writes one receipt.

Text equivalent: refuse before download on missing metadata, a hold-out match, a known URL, or no captures left; download; refuse after download on a hold-out match in the content or a known SHA-256; else commit four files under one lock.
